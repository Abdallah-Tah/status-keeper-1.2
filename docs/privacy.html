Status Keeper

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Status Keeper is a Chrome extension that keeps your chat status from switching to Away during the hours you choose. This policy explains what the extension does with your information.

The short version: Status Keeper collects nothing and sends nothing. Your settings stay in your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. Status Keeper does not collect, store on any server, sell, share or transmit any personal information, browsing history, messages, or usage data. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads. The extension does not use remote code.

What stays in your browser

Status Keeper saves these settings using Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local), on your own computer only:

  • Whether the extension is on or off
  • Your work days, start and end time, and lunch break
  • A running quick timer, if you started one
  • Whether to pause when your computer is locked
  • How often it nudges (every 1, 2 or 4 minutes)
  • How long it has been active today, shown in the popup

This data never leaves your browser. Removing the extension deletes it.

Permissions and why we need them

  • storage: saves the settings listed above in your browser.
  • alarms: checks every 30 seconds whether the extension should be active, based on your work hours and timer.
  • idle: detects when your computer is locked, so the extension pauses and resumes when you come back. It only reads whether the computer is locked or not, nothing else.
  • Host access to teams.microsoft.com, teams.cloud.microsoft and teams.live.com: runs a small script on that page that sends a light, simulated mouse movement, and lets the popup find the open tab. The script does not read, record or send your messages, contacts or any page content. It runs on no other sites.

Third parties

Status Keeper does not share any data with third parties, because it has none to share.

Children

Status Keeper is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
