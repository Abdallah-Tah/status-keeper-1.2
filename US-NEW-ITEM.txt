Description:

Status Keeper stops your status from flipping to Away when you step back from the keyboard for a few minutes, for example while reading a long document, on a phone call, or watching a training video. It only runs during the hours you choose.

What it does:
- Work hours: pick your days, start and end time, and a lunch break. Status Keeper starts and stops by itself.
- Quick timers: keep your status active for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours, then it goes back to your schedule.
- Pauses when your computer is locked, and resumes when you unlock it.
- Choose how often it nudges: every 1, 2 or 4 minutes.
- Shows what it is doing right now, when it stops, and how long it has been active today.
- Light and dark mode.

How it works: it sends a small, harmless mouse movement to the open chat tab at the interval you choose. It runs only on that page.

Privacy: no account, no sign-in, no data collected. Your settings are stored only in your browser.

Compatibility: works with Microsoft Teams in the browser.

Status Keeper is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Microsoft Corporation. Microsoft Teams is a trademark of Microsoft Corporation.

Single purpose description:
Keeps the user's status active in a web chat tab during the hours the user chooses, by sending small simulated mouse movements to that tab.

Saves the user's settings (work hours, timer, nudge interval) and today's active time in the browser.

alarms justification:
Checks every 30 seconds whether the extension should be active, based on the user's work hours and timer.

Host permission justification:
